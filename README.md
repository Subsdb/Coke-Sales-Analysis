# Coca-Cola Retail Sales & Business Performance Analysis (2021)

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Coca-Cola retail sales data for 2021 to evaluate business performance, revenue trends, product profitability and regional sales distribution.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through structured data analysis, KPI development and management reporting.

The analysis explores sales performance across different beverage brands, retailers and geographical regions, with an emphasis on identifying revenue drivers, evaluating profitability and supporting evidence-based business decisions.

## Business Objectives

The project aims to answer the following business questions:

- How does total revenue change across different months?
- Which beverage brands contribute the most to overall revenue and operating profit?
- Which geographical regions generate the highest sales volume?
- How do pricing and units sold influence revenue performance?
- Which brands demonstrate stronger operating margins?
- Are there identifiable seasonal patterns in product sales?
- Which retailers contribute significantly to overall business performance?

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains 3,888 transactional records covering the period January–December 2021.

Key attributes include:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Retailer | Name of the retail partner |
| Retailer ID | Unique retailer identifier |
| Invoice Date | Transaction date |
| Region | Geographical sales region |
| State | State where the transaction occurred |
| City | Transaction city |
| Beverage Brand | Product category |
| Price per Unit | Selling price per unit |
| Units Sold | Number of units sold |
| Total Sales | Total transaction revenue |
| Operating Profit | Profit generated from the transaction |
| Operating Margin | Operating margin percentage |
| Month | Month extracted from invoice date |

**Beverage brands covered:** Coca-Cola, Diet Coke, Sprite, Fanta, Powerade and Dasani Water.

**Geographical coverage:** Midwest, Northeast, South, Southeast and West.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following KPIs are considered to evaluate business performance:

1. **Total Revenue:** Aggregate sales generated across all transactions.
2. **Total Units Sold:** Total quantity of beverages sold.
3. **Operating Profit:** Total operating profit generated.
4. **Operating Margin:** Operating profit as a percentage of revenue.
5. **Average Selling Price:** Average revenue generated per unit sold.
6. **Monthly Revenue Growth:** Percentage change in revenue compared with the previous month.
7. **Brand Revenue Contribution:** Percentage contribution of individual brands to total revenue.
8. **Regional Sales Contribution:** Percentage of total revenue generated by each geographical region.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Preparation and Cleaning
- Reviewed the dataset structure and field definitions.
- Examined date, numerical and categorical fields.
- Reviewed consistency of product and geographical attributes.
- Prepared data for aggregation and business analysis.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Evaluated monthly revenue trends.
- Compared sales performance across beverage brands.
- Analysed sales distribution across geographical regions.
- Examined retailer-level sales contributions.

### 3. Revenue and Profitability Analysis
- Evaluated revenue contribution by beverage brand.
- Compared operating profit across product categories.
- Analysed operating margins to understand profitability.
- Examined the relationship between selling price and sales volume.

### 4. Regional Performance Analysis
- Compared units sold across geographical regions.
- Identified differences in regional sales performance.
- Evaluated geographical contributions to overall business performance.

### 5. Business Insights and Recommendations
The analysis is intended to translate numerical findings into actionable recommendations, including:
- Identifying high-performing and underperforming beverage brands.
- Understanding regional sales opportunities.
- Evaluating product profitability and margin efficiency.
- Supporting pricing and sales strategy decisions.
- Identifying monthly trends that may influence inventory and sales planning.
